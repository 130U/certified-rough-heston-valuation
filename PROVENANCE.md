# Project chronology and attribution

The earlier manuscript records that research began in the second half of 2023, the main writing took place in the first half of 2024, and some final work was completed in 2026, when the paper and code were uploaded to GitHub. This author-provided chronology is retained from its former Appendix H and the repository introduction at [baseline 183ec258](https://github.com/130U/certified-rough-heston-valuation/commit/183ec258).

The present manuscript was reorganized on **26 October 2026**. That date identifies the current revision of the exposition and publication materials. The earlier chronology describes the project's origins; it does not date every result, extension, reference or computation in the current version. In particular, material citing work from 2025 or 2026 belongs to the later development of the manuscript.

The current order follows the mathematical dependencies: the valuation question, exact model, residual identity, history kernel and joint portfolio error set. It is a presentation of the completed argument, without assigning undocumented dates or a reconstructed sequence of discoveries to those steps. [REVISION.md](REVISION.md) distinguishes the new explanations from results and calculations carried forward.

The rational approximation and endpoint-matching method used for the principal production output are due to Gatheral and Radoicic. The manuscript attributes the Volterra model, Caputo comparison tools, Mittag–Leffler positivity and Fourier quadrature analysis to their respective sources. The finite-history pricing composition, joint error certificate and specified structural-domain argument are presented with their individual assumptions and dependencies.

The classical Heston extensions formerly included as Appendices F–G are preserved in [English](supplement/classical-heston-en.md) and [Chinese](supplement/classical-heston-zh.md). Their original limitations remain part of their record. The research chronology and these supplements are separate from the assumptions needed for the main rough Heston price certificate.
