The complete parent cold time includes process startup and NumPy import; the internal continuous-loop timer begins after startup initialization. OMP/OpenBLAS/MKL/NumExpr were pinned to one thread. Python 3.12.14 and NumPy 2.3.5 were used. The residual generator uses outward binary64 intervals, while reference and propagation decisions use shared 100-bit dyadic intervals and exact rational comparisons.

`omission-verify.py` reads every one of the 4,192,768 saved residual entries, checks exact closed-cell coverage against the original field grid, every column maximum, source identities, physical normalization and all startup/later half-plane records. A separate exact generalized-power Caputo startup assembly passed all 512 first cells; six negative controls were rejected. This reader is a complete consistency audit of the saved derivative envelope bank. It does not independently derive every later derivative enclosure from scratch. The executed original generator and its recorded instrumentation remain part of the trust boundary.

`omission-aggregate.py` reconstructs the old reference exactly before assembling the new all-1025-node reference. It explicitly moves the signed centre from approximately −0.166762218 to −0.081698202 index points, retaining a +0.085064016-point correction. New high-node radii are centred at their actual nonzero stored transforms. The safe old-disk translation is old radius plus stored-transform modulus. Each new exponent-error bound includes physical residual divided by ν and full-history curve weights. Original strip and true infinite-tail budgets remain, and new reference rounding is included.

The complete new high-global and high-time-bin joint bounds are 0.132245064 and 0.115215934 index points, rounded upward; both pass the fixed 1/0.5/0.25 budgets. Their matched signed marginal bounds are 0.158585551 and 0.137896018 and also pass all three budgets. Low nodes retain the original time-local radii in both scenarios. The quarter-point success therefore cannot be attributed wholly to shared-node geometry: certifying the omitted finite nodes and moving the reference centre account for the main change. Shared geometry adds an approximately 16.6%/16.4% reduction within the respective matched new-disk comparisons.

The independent checker `independent-omission.py` does not import either omission aggregator or verifier. It reconstructs all low/high CF radii and the complete reference with separate rational arithmetic, uses an alternative sine-identity coefficient enclosure, checks the whole saved cell partition and 128 closed-bin maxima, and rejects eight negative controls. Its final executed receipt is `independent-omission.json`, SHA-256 `2b2d3c0a659e8769ed8280dc372b5d8feac672e94ac3c326a93b42540fc09d6a`. Shared scalar interval/moment/trigonometric primitives are disclosed, so this is not an assertion of completely independent numerical libraries.
