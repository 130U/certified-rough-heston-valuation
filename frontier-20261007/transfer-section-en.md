These are pricing tasks under the existing admissible model; no new market quotes or calibration conclusions are introduced. Appendix A's model, affine-transform and martingale argument applies at each finite horizon.

The previously certified continuous field and physical residual cover \([0,1/2]\). Their restrictions to \([0,1/4]\) retain the same Caputo history from zero and the same whole-time half-plane bounds. The quarter maturity is inside a stored affine time cell for each candidate. Write \(\widehat G_{\rm lin}\) for the piecewise affine remainder stored as `LG`; the fractional polynomial components retain their original analytical form. If the endpoints are \(t_\ell<T<t_{\ell+1}\), the terminal remainder is evaluated exactly as

\[
\widehat G_{\rm lin}(T,u)=(1-r)\widehat G_{\rm lin}(t_\ell,u)+r\widehat G_{\rm lin}(t_{\ell+1},u),
\qquad r=\frac{T-t_\ell}{t_{\ell+1}-t_\ell}.
\tag{TR1}
\]

All stored binary64 endpoints are interpreted as exact rationals, so this operation preserves the original continuous reference rather than constructing a new solution. The restricted hat-function weights, fractional power moments, reference exponents and exponential enclosures are recalculated at the new maturity. The exponent error uses the smaller of the finite-history bound \(\delta_FJ_0(1/4)\) and the earlier state bound propagated with the newly evaluated quarter-year curve mass.
