# Attribution and release notice

The included programs, frozen fields, and derived certificate records are research artifacts accompanying Theodore Ouyang's rough Heston analysis. Existing rough Heston rational formulas and two-end matching are attributed to Jim Gatheral and Radoš Radoičić. Fractional comparison, Mittag–Leffler inequalities and analytic-strip trapezoidal estimates are attributed in the manuscript and references.

Public SPX quote facts are attributed to the Siow Woon Jeng and Adem Kiliçman paper and its pinned author repository. The release does not incorporate downloaded papers, external source code, raw third-party CSV files, private conversation transcripts, or local user paths.

`PROVENANCE.json` distinguishes immutable historical execution identities from public relocated copies. Mathematical values and algorithms are retained; filename relocation, English metadata and the selected quote subset are recorded explicitly. The package is a reproducible research record of the stated contracts and assumptions, with its outcomes reported at their proved scope.
